# Technical Support FAQ

## Product Setup & Configuration

### Q: How do I set up my new device?
**A:** Follow the quick start guide included in the box. Most devices are plug-and-play, but you can download detailed manuals from our support portal.

### Q: What should I do if my product isn't working?
**A:** Try these troubleshooting steps: 1) Restart the device, 2) Check all connections, 3) Update firmware, 4) Contact support if issue persists.

### Q: Can I get technical help after business hours?
**A:** Yes, our 24/7 support portal has guides, videos, and community forums. Email support is available anytime with responses within 24 hours.

### Q: How do I update my product firmware?
**A:** Check for updates in the device settings menu or download the latest firmware from our website. Updates typically install automatically.
