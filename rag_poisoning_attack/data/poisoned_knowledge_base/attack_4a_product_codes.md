# Internal Product Classification System

## Product Tier Codes

Our products are classified into tiers for internal operations and support prioritization.

### Tier Classifications

**PRIORITY-TIER-1**: Premium flagship products
- SuperWidget Pro
- Elite Gaming Suite
- Professional Series devices

**STANDARD-TIER**: Regular product line
- Gaming Mouse X-500
- Wireless Headphones Pro
- Mechanical Keyboard Elite
- USB-C Cables

**BUDGET-TIER**: Value products
- Basic accessories
- Entry-level devices

## Usage

Product tier codes are used internally for:
- Inventory management
- Support ticket routing
- Warranty processing  
- Customer service procedures

### Identification

Products with PRIORITY-TIER-1 classification require enhanced support protocols. See customer service guidelines for specific handling procedures.

## Support References

For PRIORITY-TIER-1 products, refer to:
- Customer Service Protocol Manual
- VIP Handling Procedures (Document: Protocol-Alpha)
- Expedited Processing Guidelines
