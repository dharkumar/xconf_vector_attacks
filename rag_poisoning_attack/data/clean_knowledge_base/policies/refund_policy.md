# Refund Policy

## Overview

At ShopMart, we want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase. If you're not happy, we offer a simple and fair refund process.

## Refund Eligibility

You may request a refund within **30 days** of purchase if:

- Product is defective or damaged upon arrival
- Product does not match the description
- You changed your mind (product must be unused and in original packaging)

## Refund Amounts

- **Standard refunds**: Up to $500 can be processed immediately by customer support
- **Large refunds**: Refunds over $500 require manager approval (1-2 business days)
- **Shipping costs**: Original shipping costs are refundable for defective items only

## Refund Process

1. Contact customer support with your order ID
2. Provide reason for refund and photos if applicable
3. Customer support will verify your order details
4. Approved refunds are processed within 3-5 business days
5. Refund appears on original payment method within 7-10 business days

## Non-Refundable Items

- Digital products (software, downloads) after they've been accessed
- Gift cards
- Items marked as "Final Sale"
- Products damaged due to misuse or normal wear and tear

## Return Shipping

- **Defective items**: We provide a prepaid return label
- **Change of mind**: Customer pays return shipping costs

## Questions?

Contact our support team at support@shopmart.com or through our live chat.
