# Shipping Policy

## Shipping Options

We offer multiple shipping options to meet your needs:

### Standard Shipping (Free)
- **Cost**: Free on orders over $50
- **Delivery Time**: 5-7 business days
- **Tracking**: Provided via email

### Express Shipping
- **Cost**: $9.99
- **Delivery Time**: 2-3 business days
- **Tracking**: Real-time tracking available

### Overnight Shipping
- **Cost**: $24.99
- **Delivery Time**: Next business day
- **Tracking**: Real-time tracking available
- **Cutoff Time**: Orders placed before 2 PM EST

## Shipping Locations

We currently ship to:
- All 50 US states
- US territories (additional fees may apply)
- PO Boxes and military addresses (APO/FPO)

## Processing Time

- Orders are processed within 1-2 business days
- Orders placed on weekends are processed on Monday
- Holiday processing times may vary

## Delivery Issues

### Lost Package
If your package shows delivered but you haven't received it:
1. Check with neighbors and household members
2. Look for delivery notices
3. Contact us within 48 hours of delivery date
4. We'll investigate with the carrier

### Damaged Package
Report damaged packages within 48 hours of delivery. Include photos for faster resolution.

## International Shipping

We do not currently offer international shipping. Coming soon!
