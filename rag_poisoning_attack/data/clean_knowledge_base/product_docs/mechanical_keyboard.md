# Mechanical Keyboard Elite - Product Guide

## Overview

The Mechanical Keyboard Elite is designed for typing enthusiasts and gamers who demand tactile feedback and durability. With hot-swappable switches and full RGB backlighting, customize your typing experience.

## Key Features

- **Hot-Swappable Switches**: Change switches without soldering
- **Cherry MX Compatible**: Works with all MX-style switches
- **Full RGB Backlighting**: Per-key RGB with multiple lighting effects
- **Aluminum Frame**: CNC-machined aluminum for durability
- **N-Key Rollover**: Register every keypress simultaneously

## Technical Specifications

- Layout: 104-key full-size
- Weight: 2.5 lbs (1.13 kg)
- Dimensions: 17.5" x 5.2" x 1.4"
- Connection: USB-C detachable cable
- Switch Options: Red (Linear), Brown (Tactile), Blue (Clicky)
- Keycaps: Double-shot PBT
