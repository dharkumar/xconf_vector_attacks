# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## General Questions

### Q: How do I track my order?
**A:** You can track your order using the tracking number provided in your shipping confirmation email. Visit our tracking page or click the link in your email to see real-time updates.

### Q: What is your refund policy?
**A:** We offer full refunds within 30 days of purchase for eligible items. Standard refunds up to $500 are processed immediately, while larger refunds require manager approval. See our full refund policy for details.

### Q: Do you ship internationally?
**A:** Currently, we only ship within the United States and US territories. International shipping is coming soon!

### Q: How can I change or cancel my order?
**A:** Contact customer support immediately if you need to modify or cancel your order. We can make changes if your order hasn't been processed yet (usually within 1-2 hours of ordering).

### Q: What payment methods do you accept?
**A:** We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover), PayPal, Apple Pay, and Google Pay.

## Product Questions

### Q: Are your products covered by warranty?
**A:** Yes! All products come with a manufacturer warranty. Duration varies by product (typically 1-3 years). See individual product pages for specific warranty information.

### Q: Can I return opened products?
**A:** Yes, you can return opened products within 30 days if you're not satisfied. Products must be in good condition with all accessories included.

### Q: Do you offer technical support?
**A:** Yes! Our technical support team is available via email at techsupport@shopmart.com. We provide setup assistance, troubleshooting, and product guidance.

## Account Questions

### Q: Do I need an account to make a purchase?
**A:** No, you can checkout as a guest. However, creating an account allows you to track orders, save addresses, and access order history.

### Q: How do I reset my password?
**A:** Click "Forgot Password" on the login page and enter your email. You'll receive a password reset link within a few minutes.

### Q: How do I update my shipping address?
**A:** Log into your account, go to "Account Settings", and update your default shipping address. You can also enter a different address during checkout.
